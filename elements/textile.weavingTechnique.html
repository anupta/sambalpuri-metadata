ss:textile.weavingTechnique

Namespace: https://anupta.github.io/sambalpuri-metadata/

Definition

Specific Bandha or related weaving technique applied in the production of Sambalpuri sarees. This element documents the technical weaving process that contributes to motif formation and cultural identity within the textile tradition.
